WALL-FAMILY MASTER RULE

Full Wall V1 is the visual master reference for the entire wall family.

Wall Corner V1, Broken Wall V1, Doorway V1, Wall End Cap V1, Pillar V1, Archway V1, Gate / Portcullis V1, and future wall extensions must appear to be constructed from the same physical wall system.

They must share:
- chunky masonry
- bluish-gray stone
- capstones
- wood reinforcement
- brass/metal bands and hardware
- proportions
- moss/ground treatment
- tabletop scale

Do NOT redesign the wall for modular extensions.

VISUAL LANGUAGE

The entire collection uses:

- stylized hand-painted 3D appearance
- strong readable silhouettes
- slightly exaggerated proportions
- chunky tactile geometry
- premium fantasy tabletop miniature presentation
- bluish-gray stone
- dark/weathered wood
- restrained brass/bronze hardware
- moss, grass, dirt and subtle environmental wear
- consistent miniature scale/base treatment
- dramatic but controlled studio lighting

The assets are intentionally stylized.

Do NOT shift the project toward photorealism.
