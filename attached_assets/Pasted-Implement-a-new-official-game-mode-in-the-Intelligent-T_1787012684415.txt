Implement a new official game mode in the Intelligent Tabletop platform called:

AGES OF EMPIRE

This is a new territory-conquest strategy game mode inspired by classic Risk-style board-game mechanics and lightweight Civilization-style historical progression.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT replace, delete, or break the existing tabletop tactical combat game.
- Do NOT rewrite the existing engine unnecessarily.
- Inspect the repository architecture first and integrate Ages of Empire using the project's existing patterns.
- Preserve the existing content/rules/intent/UI separation where practical.
- Ages of Empire should have its own deterministic rules domain rather than injecting territory-game logic into the existing tactical combat rules.
- Keep the implementation data-driven so balance values can be changed later without rewriting rules logic.
- Use seeded deterministic RNG for all dice/combat randomness.
- Follow the repository's existing TypeScript conventions and testing approach.
- Do not build a complicated Civilization clone. This is fundamentally a classic territory-conquest tabletop game with a lightweight civilization progression layer.

==================================================
GAME IDENTITY
==================================================

Ages of Empire is:

Classic Risk-style territory conquest
+
Light Civilization-style development
+
Four timed historical Ages
+
Modern intelligent tabletop presentation.

Core turn loop:

REINFORCE → DEVELOP → ATTACK → FORTIFY → END TURN

The physical tabletop/board remains the primary interface. The digital layer handles bookkeeping, rules enforcement, timers, combat resolution, scoring, and valid-action logic.

==================================================
STANDARD GAME
==================================================

Players:
- 2–6 players
- Target experience: 4–5 players

Standard game:
- 60 minutes total
- 4 Ages
- 15 minutes per Age

Ages:
1. Ancient
2. Medieval
3. Industrial
4. Modern

The Age timer is real-time.

IMPORTANT TIMING RULE:
When an Age timer expires, do NOT switch Ages in the middle of a round.

Instead:
- Finish the current round.
- Advance the Age.
- Unlock the next Age's content.
- Begin the next round under the new Age.

Initiative rotates each round.

Example:
Round 1:
Blue → Red → Green → Yellow

Round 2:
Red → Green → Yellow → Blue

Continue rotating throughout the game.

==================================================
TERRITORIES
==================================================

Every territory should contain or expose:

- id
- name
- region
- adjacent territories
- owner
- units
- development
- resource
- capital status
- city status
- fort status
- road/railway status
- economic/production value
- victory point value

Do NOT use a grid-based tactical combat system.

The world map itself is the strategic space.

==================================================
REGIONS
==================================================

Several territories form larger regions.

Controlling an entire region grants a regional reinforcement bonus.

Regional bonus values:

- Small region: +2 Reinforcements
- Medium region: +3 Reinforcements
- Large region: +5 Reinforcements

The exact region definitions should remain data-driven.

==================================================
CAPITALS
==================================================

Each player begins with one Capital.

Capital effects:
- +1 Reinforcement per turn
- +3 Victory Points at game end

Capital capture:
- Capturing another player's Capital immediately grants +2 VP.
- The original owner loses the Capital bonus.
- The original owner is NOT eliminated.
- They may establish a new Capital after their next turn.
- New Capital costs 4 Production.

A player is eliminated only when they control zero territories.

First-round protection:
- A player's Capital cannot be attacked during the first full round.

==================================================
REINFORCEMENTS
==================================================

At the beginning of a player's turn:

Base Reinforcements =
floor(territories controlled / 3)

Minimum = 3

Then add:
- Region bonuses
- Capital bonus
- Food resource bonuses
- Other explicitly defined bonuses

Reinforcements are used primarily to recruit/upgrade military units.

==================================================
PRODUCTION
==================================================

Production is separate from Reinforcements.

Production is used for:
- Cities
- Forts
- Roads
- Railways
- Factories
- Other civilization infrastructure

Production income:

Normal territory:
+1 Production

Developed territory:
+2 Production

City:
+3 Production

Factory:
+2 Production

Production is bankable.

Maximum stored Production:
10

==================================================
DEVELOPMENT
==================================================

Development is intentionally simple.

Three major development categories:

MILITARY:
- Fort

ECONOMIC:
- City

TRANSPORTATION:
- Road
- Railway

The goal is to choose between:
- More economy
- More defense
- More mobility

Do not implement a large building tree.

==================================================
CITIES
==================================================

City cost:
6 Production

Effects:
- +2 Production
- +1 defensive die modifier
- +2 VP

Limit:
- One City per territory

Cities remain on the board when ownership changes.

The new owner receives the City's benefits.

==================================================
FORTS
==================================================

Fort cost:
4 Production

Effect:
- +1 to defender's highest combat die

Limit:
- One Fort per territory

Do not make Forts dramatically stronger than this.

==================================================
ROADS
==================================================

Road cost:
2 Production

Effect:
- Enables movement through connected friendly territory at up to 2 territory hops.

Roads can later be upgraded to Railways.

==================================================
RAILWAYS
==================================================

Available:
Industrial Age

Road → Railway upgrade:
3 Production

Railway effect:
- Armies may move between connected Railway territories during Fortify.
- Treat a connected Railway network as a long-distance movement network.

Keep the rule deterministic and simple.

==================================================
FACTORIES
==================================================

Available:
Industrial Age

Factory cost:
8 Production

Effect:
+2 Production

Limit:
3 Factories per player

==================================================
RESOURCES
==================================================

Use four resource types initially:

FOOD:
+1 Reinforcement

IRON:
One military unit recruited per turn costs 1 less Production if applicable.

GOLD:
+2 Production

OIL:
Tanks and Modern Armor gain +1 Movement.

Resources are NOT stored as currencies.

Control the resource territory and receive its benefit.

==================================================
UNIT SYSTEM
==================================================

Units remain territory-based army pieces.

Do NOT implement individual tactical units on a combat grid.

Unit data must be centralized and data-driven.

UNIT DEFINITIONS:

Ancient Age:

Infantry
- Cost: 1 Reinforcement
- Movement: 1
- Standard combat
- No modifier

Cavalry
- Cost: 2 Reinforcements
- Movement: 2
- +1 to highest attack die

Medieval Age:

Spearmen
- Cost: 1 Reinforcement
- Movement: 1
- +1 defensive die against Cavalry or Knights

Knight
- Cost: 3 Reinforcements
- Movement: 2
- +1 to highest attack die

Industrial Age:

Rifleman
- Cost: 2 Reinforcements
- Movement: 1
- One attack die may be rerolled once per battle

Artillery
- Cost: 3 Reinforcements
- Movement: 1
- +1 to highest attack die when attacking a Fort

Tank
- Cost: 4 Reinforcements
- Movement: 2
- +1 to highest attack die

Modern Age:

Mechanized Infantry
- Cost: 3 Reinforcements
- Movement: 3
- One attack die may be rerolled once per battle

Modern Armor
- Cost: 5 Reinforcements
- Movement: 4
- +1 to highest attack die
- That modified die wins ties

Air Support:
- Not a normal army unit.
- Strategic limited-use ability.
- Each player receives 2 Air Support charges when Modern Age begins.

Older units remain usable after newer units unlock.

==================================================
UNIT UPGRADES
==================================================

Units may be upgraded rather than discarded.

Upgrade cost:

Difference between old and new unit Reinforcement cost
+
1 Production

Examples:

Infantry → Rifleman:
- 1 Reinforcement
- 1 Production

Cavalry → Tank:
- 2 Reinforcements
- 1 Production

Rifleman → Mechanized Infantry:
- 1 Reinforcement
- 1 Production

Make upgrade paths data-driven.

Do not force automatic upgrading at Age transitions.

==================================================
MOVEMENT
==================================================

Movement occurs during the Fortify phase.

A player gets ONE Fortify movement action per turn.

Movement can only pass through friendly territory.

Ancient:
- Standard movement is 1 territory hop.

Medieval:
- Roads permit movement up to 2 territory hops.

Industrial:
- Railways permit long-distance movement through connected Railway networks.

Modern:
- Unit-specific movement values apply.

Movement and attacking remain separate systems.

Moving an army does NOT automatically allow it to attack.

==================================================
COMBAT
==================================================

Combat should closely preserve the classic Risk-style structure.

Attacker:
- 1 attacking unit → 1 die
- 2 attacking units → 2 dice
- 3+ attacking units → 3 dice maximum

Defender:
- 1 defending unit → 1 die
- 2+ defending units → 2 dice maximum

Sort dice highest-to-lowest.

Compare:
- highest attacker vs highest defender
- second-highest attacker vs second-highest defender if both exist

The loser of each comparison loses 1 unit.

Maximum casualties per combat round:
2

Ties:
- Defender wins.

After every combat round, the attacker chooses:
- Continue
- Withdraw

If Withdraw:
- Battle ends.
- Surviving attacking forces remain in the attacking territory.
- That territory cannot initiate another attack during the same Attack phase.

An attacker must always leave at least 1 unit behind.

A territory with only 1 unit cannot attack.

==================================================
COMBAT MODIFIERS
==================================================

All combat modifiers must be explicit and data-driven.

General rule:
- A single die cannot exceed 6.
- No ordinary modifier should exceed +1 from a single source.

Unit modifiers:

Infantry:
- None

Cavalry:
- +1 highest attack die

Spearmen:
- +1 defensive die against Cavalry/Knights

Knight:
- +1 highest attack die

Rifleman:
- One attack die reroll

Artillery:
- +1 highest attack die when attacking Fort

Tank:
- +1 highest attack die

Mechanized Infantry:
- One attack die reroll

Modern Armor:
- +1 highest attack die
- Modified die wins ties

Fort:
- +1 highest defensive die

City:
- +1 defensive die

Combined Arms:
If attacking force contains at least 3 different unit types:
- +1 to one attack die
- Does not stack

Do not allow modifiers to create runaway combat values.

==================================================
CAPTURE
==================================================

When defending units reach zero:
- Territory changes ownership.
- Attacking units from the final attack move into the territory.
- Territory's City/Fort/Resource benefits transfer to the new owner.
- Regional control recalculates.
- VP recalculates.
- Conquest card eligibility is checked.

Capturing at least one player-controlled territory during a turn:
- Draw 1 Conquest Card.

==================================================
CONQUEST CARDS
==================================================

Maximum hand:
5 cards.

Cards:

Reinforcement:
+3 Reinforcements

Forced March:
+2 movement for one movement action

Defensive Mobilization:
+1 defense for one territory during the current combat round

Rapid Deployment:
Allows one additional army group movement during Fortify

Artillery Support:
+1 attack die modifier for one battle

Emergency Defense:
Immediately move up to 3 armies into a threatened friendly territory

Cards should be limited-use.

Do not make them game-ending.

Card sets:

Three matching cards:
+4 Reinforcements

Three different cards:
+6 Reinforcements

Cards can be turned in at the beginning of Reinforce.

==================================================
AGE TRANSITION BONUSES
==================================================

When an Age ends, award:

Ancient → Medieval:
Most territories:
+2 VP

Medieval → Industrial:
Most Cities:
+2 VP

Industrial → Modern:
Largest Railway network:
+2 VP

Modern:
No Age transition bonus.

Do not award excessive VP for Age dominance.

==================================================
AIR SUPPORT
==================================================

At the start of Modern Age:
Each player receives 2 Air Support charges.

Each charge may be spent for one strategic effect:
- +1 attack die
- +1 defensive die
- Limited distant strike
- Special redeployment

Exact effects should be represented by a typed action/effect system.

No production/building system for aircraft.

==================================================
DIPLOMACY
==================================================

Diplomacy is lightweight.

Players may negotiate freely.

Optional formal Treaty:
- Lasts 1 round.
- Prevents direct attacks between the two parties during that round.
- Does not prevent movement.
- Does not prevent attacks against third parties.
- No automatic punishment for breaking a social agreement after the treaty expires.

Do not create a complicated diplomacy UI.

==================================================
NEUTRAL TERRITORIES
==================================================

Neutral territories:
- Start with 2–4 Infantry.
- Do not expand.
- Defend themselves.

Capturing a neutral territory:
- Provides territory normally.
- Provides normal economic/resource benefits.
- Does not count as attacking another player for objectives that specifically require player-vs-player aggression.

==================================================
OBJECTIVES
==================================================

Each player receives 3 objectives.

Completing an objective:
+4 VP

Maximum objective VP:
12

Objective examples:
- Control 3 regions.
- Control 5 Cities.
- Control a specific strategic region.
- Hold your Capital until game end.
- Control 4 resource territories.
- Build a specified infrastructure network.

Objectives must be data-driven.

Do not make objectives impossible based on player count.

==================================================
VICTORY POINTS
==================================================

Baseline scoring:

Territory:
+1 VP

Region:
+5 VP

City:
+2 VP

Capital:
+3 VP

Resource territory:
+1 VP

Completed objective:
+4 VP

Capturing an enemy Capital:
+2 immediate VP

Current VP should be visible during the game.

Final VP should be calculated deterministically at game end.

==================================================
VICTORY
==================================================

When the Modern Age timer expires:

1. Finish the current round.
2. Stop normal gameplay.
3. Calculate final Victory Points.
4. Determine winner.

Do NOT require total world conquest.

Tiebreakers:
1. Most territories
2. Most Cities
3. Most total military units
4. Most completed objectives
5. If still tied: shared victory
