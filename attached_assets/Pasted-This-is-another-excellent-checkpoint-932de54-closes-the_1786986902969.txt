Combatant.id
    = encounter-local identity
Combatant.defId
    = content/template identity
Combatant.worldId
    = persistent-world identity

WorldEntity

defId   = "orc-warrior"
wx      = 31
wy      = 20

may become:

Combatant
id      = "combatant-3"
defId   = "orc-warrior"
worldId = "orc-17"
wx      = 31
wy      = 20

The two IDs must never be conflated.
