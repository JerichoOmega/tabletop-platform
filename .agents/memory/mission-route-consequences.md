---
name: Mission route consequences
description: Durable design rule for making authored RPG mission routes mechanically distinct.
---

Mission route differences belong in the existing mission state and exploration boundary: route profiles should control traversal length/exposure, while route-specific interactions can grant a small existing RPG reward and change the authored climax state.

**Why:** A route choice is not meaningful if it only changes a label or completes an objective immediately. The platform’s finite mission contract can express meaningful risk/reward without adding a general quest, crafting, or inventory subsystem.

**How to apply:** Keep the direct route short and exposed with its cost outcome; keep the ridge route longer and covered, require an in-world interaction for its reward, and preserve the existing mission escalation and resolution graph.
